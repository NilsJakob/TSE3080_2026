Why is english the official course language?¶

The Norwegian Agency for Quality Assurance (Nasjonalt organ for kvalitet i utdanningen) NOKUT study in 2007 Improve internationalisation of engineering courses

  • English text books ($\rightarrow$ passive learning)
  • Courses partly in English ($\rightarrow$ active learning)
  • Whole English semester

  • Prepared for study/work abroad or in international context
  • Study more attractive to foreign students
  • Improved job opportunities
  • Easier entry to Master level

Theese and other D'frames are anaylytical frames to understand the trends governing the increasingly compex future power system. Power system data provides the facts and the underlaying numbers.

Build vs. Buy¶

  • The root of the concept originates from Ronald Coase’s 1937 paper The Nature of the Firm, which mathematically defined why companies choose to perform tasks internally ("make") versus using the open market ("buy").

  • The orange line (Off-the-shelf solution): Illustrates the ongoing effort when choosing a proprietary, ready-to-use product.

  • Time and resource(vendor lock-in), making it highly demanding and expensive to add new, customized functionality.

  • The blue line (Building from scratch):Large investment and high effort in the initial phase. Over time, however, the effort decreases significantly because the system is built on open standards and is interoperable.

  • Open-source models and agentic frameworks now allow any company to assemble what it once had to buy. What is known as the SaaSpocalypse refers to a significant downturn and valuation drop in the Software as a Service (SaaS) industry caused by AI.

  • Having the capacity to build your own software is indeed a differentiator.
